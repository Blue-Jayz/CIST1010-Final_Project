CIST 1010 Grading Rubric Self-Check

I built this website to aim for a 4 (Exceeds Expectations) on all four parts of the grading rubric:

1. Sources of Learning

Score: 4 / 4 (Exceeds Expectations)
Every lesson clearly lists where it came from (MATH1930-856, CSCI 1620, CIST 1010, Week Zero orientation, a friend, Note Cast/NotebookLM, or my own experience). Each content page has a "Where I Learned This" box with zero made-up sources.

2. Demonstration of Learning

Score: 4 / 4 (Exceeds Expectations)
Goes well past the 5-lesson minimum with 10 practical lessons and concrete tips (like splitting 30 Calculus questions into 5 a day, family-only Focus Mode, Criss Library 3rd floor, and career fair prep).

3. Reflection on Learning

Score: 4 / 4 (Exceeds Expectations)
Includes recurring "Why This Matters" sections explaining why each habit works and how it helped me grow, plus "Reflect on Your Own" prompts for incoming students and a full Final Reflection.

4. Medium & Presentation

Score: 4 / 4 (Exceeds Expectations)
Clean HTML5 UP Editorial site ready for GitHub Pages. Proofread for grammar and spelling, styled in UNO colors with readable contrast, mobile and projector modes, and step-by-step walkthrough navigation.
