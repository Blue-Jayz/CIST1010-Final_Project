Don't Wait Until Midnight: Surviving Your First Semester at UNO

College is an incredible chapter, but nobody warns you how fast a packed schedule can spiral when you are commuting, working, and trying to stay on top of college-level coursework. Here is honest, practical advice learned the hard way during my first semester as a Maverick.

Welcome, Future Mavericks

If you are reading this as an incoming freshman at UNO, welcome to the Maverick family! I created this guide as my final project for CIST 1010: College and Career Success, but more importantly, I wrote it as a gift to future students. Everything on this site is written from one student to another: friendly, transparent, and built on real experiences rather than generic clichés.

I am a first-year student who commutes 30 to 40 minutes each way, works a job, and hits the gym consistently. I know exactly what it feels like when your daily planner looks like a game of Tetris and you are wondering how you will balance rigorous STEM courses like Calculus and Computer Science II (CSCI 1620) without sacrificing your health, your friendships, or your sanity.

Core Takeaway The Single Lesson That Saves Everything

Get it done early. Your future self (and your sleep) will thank you. Procrastination feels harmless at 3:00 PM on a Tuesday, but when you are staring down a 30-question problem set at 11:30 PM with an 8:00 AM alarm waiting for you, the stress compounds fast. Taking control of your calendar early gives you freedom, confidence, and peace of mind.
