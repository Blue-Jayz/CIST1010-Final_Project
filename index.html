Don't Wait Until Midnight

A First-Year Survival Guide for Incoming UNO Students

College is an exciting transition, but nobody prepares you for how quickly a schedule spirals when you commute 30 to 40 minutes each way, work a job, go to the gym, and tackle heavy college coursework. This website is my final project for CIST 1010: College and Career Success at the University of Nebraska Omaha (UNO). It is written from one student to another: honest, transparent, and built entirely on real lessons learned the hard way.

Welcome, Future Mavericks

I am Justin Blue, a first-year student in the College of Information Science and Technology (IS&T) at UNO. I know what a packed schedule feels like. Between driving across Omaha during rush hour, working scheduled shifts, training at the gym, and taking demanding technical classes like Computer Science II (CSCI 1620) and Calculus, free time does not happen by accident.

Core Takeaway The Single Habit That Saves Everything

Get it done early. Your future self (and your sleep) will thank you. Procrastination feels harmless at 3:00 PM on a Tuesday, but when you are staring down a 30-question problem set at 11:30 PM with an 8:00 AM alarm waiting, the stress multiplies fast. Taking control of your calendar early gives you freedom, peace of mind, and genuine confidence.
