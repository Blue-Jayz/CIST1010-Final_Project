Don't Wait Until Midnight

A First-Year Survival Guide for Incoming UNO Students

College is a fun transition, but nobody really prepares you for how fast a schedule spirals when you commute 30-40 minutes each way, work a job, go to the gym, and take a lot of college coursework. This website is my final project for CIST 1010: College and Career Success at the University of Nebraska Omaha (UNO). It's written from one student to another: honest, straightforward, and built on all the lessons I learned the hard way.

Welcome, Future Mavericks

I'm Justin Blue, a first-year student in the College of Information Science and Technology (IS&T) at UNO. I know what a packed schedule feels like. Between driving across Omaha during rush hour, working shifts, going to the gym, and taking tough classes like Computer Science II (CSCI 1620) and Calculus as a first-year, free time doesn't happen by accident; I make it happen.

Core Takeaway The Single Habit That Saves Everything

Honestly, just get it done early. Your future self (and your sleep) will thank you. Putting work off feels harmless at 3:00 PM on a Tuesday, but when you're staring down a 30-question problem set at 11:30 PM with a 7:00 AM alarm waiting, the stress piles up fast. Getting control of your calendar early gives you free time, peace of mind, and actual confidence.
